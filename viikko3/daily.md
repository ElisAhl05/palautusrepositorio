# Daily scrum

During the daily scrum, there should be very little pressure put upon the team members. Each members should be comfortable presenting their achievements as well as mistakes, and no blame should be put on individual members for hardships or difficulties. It is important to keep the dialogue open and friendly as to not make team members feel uncomfortable and letting faced difficulties remain unsaid. Other members' opinions should be requested, and it is thus important to let each member state what they think in an orderly manner. To do this, one might prefer a modell where the members each alternate, saying whatever they want be said. This will ensure that everyone gets proper space and time to ventilate important issues. It is also important that members are honest about the difficulties that they face, so that these can be tackled early on.
