# Retrospective techniques

There are many alternative ways of conducting the retrospective. One of these is the Start, Stop, Continue, More of, Less of Wheel. Using this method, the team needs to decide what it wants to continue doing, stop doing, start doing, etc., just like the title says.

The Glad, Sad, Mad method instead lets each team member lift forward what happened during the sprint that made them happy, what made them feel hurt and what made them feel enraged. This helps lift forward behaviours that would otherwise not be commented upon.

There is yet the modell that works using different rounds. First, a writer is selected to write down everything that is being said during the retrospective. Then, the last retrospective's improvement proposals are gone through. Next, each member gets to think for a minute and think of one thing that went well during the sprint. These ideas are then discussed. This is repeated for things that the members want to improve, and things that they want to stop doing. This process might be repeated if there is enough time.
